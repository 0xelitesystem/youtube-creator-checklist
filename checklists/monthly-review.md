# Monthly channel review

Run on the same date each month. Half hour to an hour. Pen and paper or doc.

## Metrics review

- [ ] Total subscribers (start vs end of month)
- [ ] Total views (this month vs last month)
- [ ] Total watch hours
- [ ] Net subscriber change per video published
- [ ] Top 3 videos by views
- [ ] Bottom 3 videos by views
- [ ] CTR average across the month
- [ ] Average view duration across the month

## What worked

- [ ] Which video formats performed best?
- [ ] Which thumbnails got highest CTR?
- [ ] Which titles got highest CTR?
- [ ] Which topics had the best retention?
- [ ] Which videos brought new subscribers?

## What didn't

- [ ] Which videos under-performed and why (best guess)?
- [ ] Were there topics your audience didn't engage with?
- [ ] Were there formats you tried that didn't land?

## Audience

- [ ] Top demographics (age, region, language)
- [ ] Has your audience makeup shifted?
- [ ] Are comments revealing audience interests you should explore?

## Workflow

- [ ] How long is your average video taking to produce?
- [ ] Where are the bottlenecks? (Writing, recording, editing, thumbnail, publishing)
- [ ] Any tools / processes that could be improved or removed?

## Income (if monetized)

- [ ] AdSense revenue
- [ ] Sponsorship revenue
- [ ] Affiliate revenue
- [ ] Membership / Patreon
- [ ] Total compared to last month and last quarter

## Sponsor pipeline

- [ ] Pending sponsor inquiries
- [ ] Active sponsor relationships
- [ ] Sponsors due for renewal conversations
- [ ] Sponsors to follow up with

## Goals

- [ ] Did you meet last month's goals?
- [ ] What are this month's goals?
- [ ] Are the goals realistic given your time and current channel state?

## Burnout check

- [ ] Are you enjoying the work?
- [ ] Is the cadence sustainable?
- [ ] Is anything making you dread publishing?
- [ ] Is the channel taking time from things that matter more (health, family, primary income)?

If any of these answers concern you, address them before they become a problem. The channel is for you, not the other way around.

# Quarterly channel audit

Run once a quarter. Two hours. This is the deep one.

## Performance trends

- [ ] Subscriber growth trend over the quarter (chart it)
- [ ] View trend over the quarter
- [ ] Watch-time trend
- [ ] Are trends accelerating, plateauing, or declining?
- [ ] What's likely driving the trend?

## Content audit

- [ ] List every video published this quarter
- [ ] Mark each as: keeper / fine / underperformer
- [ ] For underperformers: identify the pattern (topic, format, thumbnail, timing)
- [ ] Decide: continue with the underperforming pattern (because of strategic value), modify it, or stop

## Catalog audit

- [ ] Are there videos in your catalog that no longer represent your current quality?
  - [ ] Decide: leave, hide (unlist), update title/thumbnail, or replace with newer video
- [ ] Are there videos with broken links in descriptions?
- [ ] Are there videos with outdated information that should have a "see updated version" pinned comment?
- [ ] Are there sponsored videos with sponsors that no longer exist or are no longer reputable?

## Branding consistency

- [ ] Does your channel banner reflect what you actually make now?
- [ ] Does your channel description?
- [ ] Are your social links current?
- [ ] Is your business contact email up to date?
- [ ] Does your trailer represent your current style?

## Playlist audit

- [ ] Are playlists organized in a way that helps viewers binge?
- [ ] Should any playlists be merged, split, or retired?
- [ ] Does the order of videos within each playlist make sense?

## Audience changes

- [ ] Has your audience demographic shifted?
- [ ] Are your top countries / languages the same as last quarter?
- [ ] Should you adjust content for the audience that's actually showing up?

## Strategic questions

- [ ] What 1-3 things, if you stopped doing, would you not miss?
- [ ] What 1-3 things, if you started doing, would meaningfully change the channel?
- [ ] Is there a content area you've been avoiding that the audience wants?
- [ ] Is there a content area you've been forcing that the audience doesn't want?

## Income / business

- [ ] Quarterly revenue breakdown by source
- [ ] Cost of production (your time + tools + assets)
- [ ] Effective hourly rate
- [ ] Is the channel viable as currently structured?

## Long-term

- [ ] Where do you want this channel in 12 months?
- [ ] Is what you're doing now consistent with that?
- [ ] What's the gap, and what's the plan to close it?

## Permission to change

- [ ] If something isn't working, you have permission to change it
- [ ] If something is working, you have permission to keep doing it
- [ ] Both decisions should be deliberate, not default

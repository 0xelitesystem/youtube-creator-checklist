# 24-hour review

Run 24 hours after publish.

## Metrics to check

- [ ] Click-through rate (CTR) on impressions
- [ ] Average view duration
- [ ] Average percentage viewed
- [ ] Audience retention curve (where do viewers drop?)
- [ ] Traffic sources (where are views coming from?)
- [ ] Subscriber gain / loss

## Compare to baseline

- [ ] How does CTR compare to your channel's average?
- [ ] How does retention compare to your channel's average for similar-length videos?
- [ ] Are these metrics surprising? Better than expected? Worse?

## If CTR is below your baseline

Possible causes:
- [ ] Thumbnail not communicating value at small sizes
- [ ] Title doesn't match what your audience expects from you
- [ ] Topic outside your channel's typical content
- [ ] Bad publish time for your audience
- [ ] Suggestions: try a thumbnail variant, edit title, give it another 48-72 hours

## If retention drops sharply at a specific moment

- [ ] Identify the moment (e.g. minute 3:45)
- [ ] Watch that moment yourself
- [ ] Likely causes: long pause, bad joke, off-topic detour, sponsor segment, technical issue
- [ ] Note for next video: avoid the pattern

## Comments

- [ ] Reply to substantive comments (criticism, questions, compliments)
- [ ] Heart comments that add value
- [ ] Pin a comment that addresses a frequent question (or the most useful viewer comment)
- [ ] Remove spam, hate, doxxing, off-topic disruption
- [ ] Don't engage with bad-faith comments, block, don't argue

## Sponsor (if applicable)

- [ ] Send sponsor the URL + first-day metrics if that's part of your agreement
- [ ] Note any sponsor-specific feedback in your tracker (for renewal discussions)

## Notes for next video

- [ ] What worked
- [ ] What didn't
- [ ] What to try next time
